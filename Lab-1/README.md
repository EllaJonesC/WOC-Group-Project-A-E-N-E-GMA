# Lab 1: Tableau Dashboard & Business Strategy

## Project Track

Track A

## Project Focus

Our team is focused on improving WOC's equipment rental and repair processes.

WOC's current system can result in equipment rentals and repairs being booked incorrectly, including damaged equipment being reserved for customers. These problems can create poor customer experiences, waste employee time, cause double bookings, and potentially result in lost revenue.

## Team Direction

Our preliminary recommendation is for WOC to implement a new inventory software system to replace its current disconnected spreadsheet system. The goal is to streamline equipment reservations and repair workflows while reducing customer dissatisfaction.

## Lab 1 Goal

For Lab 1, our team will use Tableau and business analysis to better understand the problems with WOC's current system, including the potential impact on revenue and company resources.

The results of this lab will help inform the systems and solutions we develop in later labs.
